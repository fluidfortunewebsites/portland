# Portland Applications

**A collection of applications proving Portland was a beautiful mistake.**

Live at: portland.fluidfortune.com

---

## What Is This

Portland Applications is a set of browser-based interactive experiences built around Portland, Oregon — its infrastructure history, its cultural stereotypes, its weather, its coffee density, and its deeply complicated relationship with freeways, lazy rivers, and fixed-gear bicycles.

It is absurd. It is also historically grounded. Both of these things are true simultaneously, which is itself very Portland.

---

## Applications

### 🌧️ Portland Simulator

A real-time (not actually real-time) dashboard of Portland metrics including rain saturation, coffee shop density by neighborhood, food cart tracking, fixed-gear bike aggression index, flannel temperature calculations, a weird news generator, a Powell's Books page counter, an indie band name generator, and a Keep Portland Weird™ compliance score.

**File:** `portland-simulator.html`

---

### 🛣️ Harbor Drive: A Portland Saga

A branching narrative game set in 1974 in which you are Portland's newly appointed City Commissioner. You must decide the fate of Harbor Drive (US Route 99 West), a six-lane freeway along the waterfront — and every subsequent infrastructure decision that follows, including but not limited to lazy rivers, the Mount Hood Freeway, I-205 corridor selection, the Banfield Expressway, and a complete inner-tube circuit of greater Portland using natural and artificial waterways.

Thirty-one scenes. Seventeen distinct endings. One volcanic eruption (conditional). See `HARBOR-DRIVE-HOWTOPLAY.md` for full documentation.

**File:** `harbor-drive-game.html`

## Faction System (Harbor Drive Game)

The game tracks four hidden metrics that respond to your decisions:

| Metric | Increases When | Decreases When |
|---|---|---|
| **Political Capital** | You make popular, sensible decisions | You build lazy rivers or upset constituencies |
| **Budget Health** | You choose fiscally conservative options | You build multiple lazy rivers or all the freeways |
| **Weirdness** | You make absurd, chaotic, distinctly Portland choices | You choose streetcars or act European about it |
| **Dante's Favor** | You build freeways (traffic = nightclub customers) | You build bike lanes or parks (cyclists don't drink) |

## Historical Notes

This project takes real Portland infrastructure history seriously, even when it is being ridiculous about it.

**Harbor Drive** was a real six-lane freeway along Portland's west bank waterfront. It was removed in 1974 and replaced with **Tom McCall Waterfront Park**, named for the Oregon Governor who championed the removal. This was one of the first freeway removals in American history and is widely considered a success.

**The Mount Hood Freeway** was a real proposed freeway that would have run eastbound from downtown Portland via the Division Street/Powell Boulevard corridor toward Gresham and beyond. It was cancelled in 1979 after significant community opposition, and federal funds were redirected to what became the MAX light rail system.

**The Banfield Expressway** (I-84) is real and exists today, running east from downtown Portland through the Columbia River Gorge corridor toward Hood River and beyond.

**I-205** was built along a corridor approximating the 92nd Avenue alignment described in the game, bypassing central Portland to the east. It opened in segments between 1978 and 1983.

**The Rose City Freeway, Northwest Industrial Freeway, and State Route 217 Extension** described in the game are real unbuilt proposals that were studied and abandoned at various points in Portland's planning history.

**The Inner-Tube Loop** is fictional. However, the Columbia River, the Willamette River, and the general geography described are accurate.

## Geography Notes

- **Astoria** is at the mouth of the Columbia River on the Oregon coast, approximately 95 miles northwest of Portland. U.S. Route 30 runs west from Portland through St. Helens toward Astoria.
- **Troutdale** is approximately 15 miles east of Portland near the entrance to the Columbia River Gorge. The Mount Hood Freeway would have run eastbound in this direction.
- **Gresham** is southeast of Troutdale. I-205 serves the area.
- **Milwaukie** is immediately south of Portland along the Willamette River's east bank.
- **PDX** is the correct designation for Portland International Airport.
